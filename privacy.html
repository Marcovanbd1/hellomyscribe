Privacy

This notice explains what happens to your information when you use this website. The MyScribe app will have its own privacy notice, published before the app launches. To see how the app will look after your conversations, read our Trust page.

Last updated 6 October 2026.

Who we are

MyScribe is made by Opsimath Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17461745. Opsimath Ltd is responsible for your information under UK data protection law. You can contact us about anything in this notice at hello@hellomyscribe.com.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address.

If you send us a message, we keep your name, your email address, who you're getting in touch as, and your message. Please don't include details about your health in a message. We don't need them to reply.

We don't collect anything else. This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking.

Why we use it

We use your waitlist email only to tell you when MyScribe launches and to share major news before then. We do this because you've asked us to, and you can change your mind at any time.

We use what you send in a message only to reply to you and to follow up on what you asked about. We do this because it's in both our interests that we can answer you.

We never sell your information, share it for anyone else's marketing, or use it to train AI.

Who handles it for us

Our website and forms are run by Netlify, and form notifications reach us by email through Google. Our website also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser shares your IP address with Google when you visit.

These companies may store information in the United States. Where that happens, it's protected by safeguards approved under UK law.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist email until MyScribe launches, then for up to 12 months unless you ask us to remove it sooner. We keep messages for up to 2 years, so we can follow up if you get back in touch.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. To leave the waitlist or make any of these requests, email hello@hellomyscribe.com. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
