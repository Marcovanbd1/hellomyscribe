Built in a neighbourhood, for neighbours.

MyScribe started on the Isle of Portland in Dorset, in conversations with people in libraries, cafés and pubs. People knew good help existed. They just couldn't hold on to what they'd been told.

So we built something for the moment it matters most: the conversation itself.

Mark Winterburn

Founder and Applied Philosopher

Mark is an applied philosopher working in NHS primary care in Dorset. He listens to what people actually need, then builds it. Before the NHS, he spent over twenty years in television broadcast, as a Playout Director with the BBC, Channel 4, ITV and BT Sport.

Friends since childhood

Every MyScribe decision, from the screens to the smallest wording, has been shaped by a nurse, a social worker, a counselling psychologist and an applied philosopher: four friends since childhood, from Portland and Weymouth, with over a century of professional and lived experience between them.
