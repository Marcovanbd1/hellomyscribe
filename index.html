Good questions

Am I allowed to record my appointment?

Yes. In the UK you don't need your doctor's permission to record your own appointment, because it's your own personal information. The Medical Defence Union, which advises doctors, says the same. MyScribe still opens on the question, because asking openly is the kind thing to do.

Will my doctor mind?

Asking openly makes a real difference. The BMA, the doctors' professional body, encourages doctors to support recordings made openly and with agreement, especially where patients may find it hard to remember information. If your doctor would rather you didn't, you can simply not record.

What happens to the recording?

The audio is deleted once it has been written down. The words and your summary are kept, encrypted, in your account until you delete them.

How much will it cost?

Pennies, not pounds. MyScribe is free to download and your first conversation is free. After that, you top up a little credit when you need it. No subscription, credit never runs out, and you'll never be asked to pay before or during a conversation. We'll share exact prices at launch.

What if I can't afford it?

No one should miss out on remembering their own conversation. Through our Gifted Minutes Pool, people and organisations fund minutes for those who need them, and nobody who funds them ever sees your conversations. Find out more, or help fill the pool.
