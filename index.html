Three steps. Nothing to learn.

  1. 1. Ask

    MyScribe opens on the question. Ask it out loud, so everyone in the room knows.

  2. 2. Record

    One button. Put your phone down and listen to the person in front of you.

  3. 3. Remember

    Get a Faithful Summary in plain words. Read it, have it read aloud, or share it with family.

The words, not a guess

Every line links back

Each point in your Faithful Summary takes you to the words that were actually said.

Ask what was said

“Was it one tablet or two?” MyScribe takes you straight to the answer, in the clinician's own words.

Unclear words are flagged

If something wasn't heard clearly, MyScribe marks it, so you know what to check.

No tape left behind

The audio is deleted once it's been written down. You keep the words, not the recording.
