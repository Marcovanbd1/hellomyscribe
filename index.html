For practices, PCNs and ICBs

Good questions

Am I allowed to record my appointment?

Yes. In the UK you don't need your doctor's permission to record your own appointment, because it's your own personal information. The Medical Defence Union, which advises doctors, says the same. MyScribe still opens on the question, because asking openly is the kind thing to do.

Will my doctor mind?

Asking openly makes a real difference. The BMA, the doctors' professional body, encourages doctors to support recordings made openly and with agreement, especially where patients may find it hard to remember information. If your doctor would rather you didn't, you can simply not record.

What happens to the recording?

The audio is deleted once it has been written down. You keep the words and your MySummary, not the recording.

Is the summary always right?

MySummary is written by AI, so it may miss things. That's why the full conversation is always there to check, and why the summary leaves out medicine names and doses. If anything is unclear, check the full conversation or ask your clinician.

Who can see my appointment?

Only you, and the people you choose to share it with.
