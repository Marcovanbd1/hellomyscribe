For practices, PCNs, ICBs and partners

Patients who remember need to call back less.

Patients already record appointments, often covertly. MyScribe makes it open, agreed and useful, with a faithful record the patient owns.

40-80%

of what is said in a medical appointment is forgotten almost straight away. (Kessels, 2003)

1 in 5

GP appointments are for needs that are not medical.

15 million

GP appointments are missed in England every year, costing the NHS over £216 million.

Less recontact

Fewer “what did the doctor say?” calls to reception, because the answer is on the patient's phone.

Better-informed patients

Plans, next steps and referrals in plain words, shared with the carers and family who help make them happen.

A transcript, not a tape

The audio is deleted once it's written down. Patients keep the words, with anything unclear flagged, and every line of the summary links back to what was said.

Aligned with the 10 Year Health Plan

Patient power, neighbourhood health and care closer to home, in a tool people carry with them.
