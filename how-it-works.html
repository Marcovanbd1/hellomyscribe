Record. Summarise. Remember.

That's all MyScribe does. Like a notebook or a voice recorder, it helps you remember. It doesn't diagnose, and it doesn't give advice. It writes down the words and tells the story of what happened in plain language, so you don't have to remember it all.

1. Ask

Open the app and it asks the question for you: “Do you mind if I record this? Just to help me remember.” Recording is open and agreed, never secret.

2. Record

Tap once. MyScribe listens while you listen. No forms, no settings, no training needed.

3. Remember

You get the story of your appointment in plain words, saved in My conversations. Tap any underlined part to see what was actually said. Read it again later, or have MyScribe read it aloud to you in a clear voice. Share it with a partner, a carer or family.

4. Ask Your Conversation

Not sure what you heard? Ask. “Was it one tablet or two?” MyScribe finds the moment in your full conversation and shows you the exact words that were said, or reads them aloud. This is where to find exact details like medicine names and doses, which the summary leaves out. Anything that wasn't heard clearly is flagged, so you know what to check.

Questions

Do I need to ask before recording?

In the UK you don't have to, because it's your own appointment. But MyScribe opens on the question, because asking openly keeps trust in the room. The BMA, the doctors' professional body, encourages doctors to support recordings made openly and with agreement.

Can I listen instead of reading?

Yes. MyScribe can read your summary aloud, or any part of your conversation, which helps if reading is hard, your eyesight isn't what it was, or you simply prefer to listen. You hear what was said, in a clear voice. There's no recording to replay, because the audio is deleted once it's written down.

What if it didn't catch something?

MyScribe flags any words it couldn't hear clearly, rather than guessing. If it matters, check with the person you spoke to.

Is the summary always right?

The summary is written by AI, so it may miss things. That's why the full conversation is always there to check, and why the summary leaves out medicine names and doses. If anything is unclear, check the full conversation or ask your clinician.

Is it only for GP appointments?

We're starting in health, because it's where remembering matters most. It works for any conversation that matters.

How much will it cost?

Free to download, then you only pay for what you use. We'll share pricing at launch.
