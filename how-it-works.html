Scribe it. Go back to it.

That's all MyScribe does. It writes down the words and tells the story of what happened, in plain language. It doesn't diagnose, and it doesn't give advice.

1. Ask, then record

MyScribe opens on the question: “Do you mind if I record this? Just to help me remember.” Once they agree, tap once. MyScribe listens while you listen.

2. Scribe it

You get two things, saved in My conversations:

The summary

The story of your appointment in plain words. Tap any underlined line to see what was actually said.

The doctor said your blood test came back normal.

The full conversation

Every word, written down, so you can check the finer details.

Speaker 2: “Your results came back normal, nothing to worry about.”

3. Go back to it

Come back to it whenever you need to.

Ask Your Conversation

Not sure what you heard? Ask. MyScribe shows you the exact words that were said, including medicine names and doses, which the summary leaves out.

“Was it one tablet or two?”

Share

Send the summary to a partner, a carer or family. They don't need the app to read it.

Questions

Do I need to ask before recording?

In the UK you don't have to, because it's your own appointment. But asking keeps trust in the room, and the BMA encourages doctors to support recordings made openly.

Can I listen instead of reading?

Yes. MyScribe can read the summary, or any part of the conversation, aloud in a clear voice.

Is the summary always right?

It's written by AI, so it may miss things. That's why the full conversation is always there to check, and anything that wasn't heard clearly is flagged. If in doubt, ask your clinician.

Is it only for GP appointments?

We're starting in health, where remembering matters most. It works for any conversation that matters.

How much will it cost?

Pennies, not pounds. Free to download, and your first conversation is free. After that, top up a little credit when you need it. No subscription, and your credit stays in your account while you use MyScribe. Exact prices at launch.

What if I can't afford it?

No one should miss out on remembering their own conversation. Our Gifted Minutes Pool, opening soon after launch, lets people and organisations fund minutes for those who need them.
